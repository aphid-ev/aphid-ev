The orange traction cables, the motor cables and the AC charge lead.
[[HV system]] has the schematic, and [[wiring]] explains how to read the
drawings.

# Traction bus

The battery string to the HV junction box, and the two
branches from the junction box to the [[inverter]] and the
[[obc|OBC/DC-DC]].

![Harness drawing of the HV cables between the batteries, junction box, inverter and OBC/DC-DC](/static/generated/wiring/main_hv_harness.svg#inline "Main HV harness")

# Motor

The three phase conductors and the resolver and temperature pairs between
the inverter and the [[motor]].

![Harness drawing of the three phase conductors from the inverter to the motor](/static/generated/wiring/motor_phase_harness.svg#inline "Motor phase harness")

![Harness drawing of the resolver and temperature pairs from the inverter control connector to the motor](/static/generated/wiring/motor_feedback_harness.svg#inline "Motor feedback harness")

# AC charge lead

Line, neutral and protective earth from the [[obc#inlet|charge inlet]] to
the AC connector of the OBC/DC-DC.

![Harness drawing of the AC lead from the charge inlet to the OBC](/static/generated/wiring/ac_charge_harness.svg#inline "AC charge harness")
