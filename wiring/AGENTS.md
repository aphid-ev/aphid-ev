# Wiring guidelines

wirebug model of the vehicle's electrical system. The `.wb` language is
documented in the `wirebug-dsl` skill under `.claude/skills/`, generated by
`just wiring skill` for the pinned wirebug. Read it before editing `.wb` files
and write nothing it does not show. After bumping `wirebug_version` in the root
justfile, run `just wiring skill`, `just wiring fmt` and `just wiring check`,
and commit the regenerated skill with the upgrade.

The site's wiki documents the electrical design behind the model: the
components, 12 V distribution, CAN topology, operating states and the safety
concept. The pages are `hv-system`, `lv-system`, `batteries`, `bms`,
`contactors`, `obc`, `ev-can`, `safety` and `vcm` under `site/content/wiki/`. Read the relevant page before changing
a system, and update it in the same commit when the change alters the design.

## Layout

The whole vehicle is one component, `Vehicle`. `main.wb` declares it and pulls
every other file in with `fragment` lines; each of those files is an
`extend Vehicle { … }` fragment. Fragments merge into one flat namespace, so a
wire or view in one file can name an instance declared in another.

- `systems/*.wb`: one logical system each. It owns the instances it introduces,
  the wires that carry its signals, and its schematic views.
- `looms/*.wb`: one physical bundle each. It owns the cables, the `inline`
  connectors at its branch points, and its harness views. A conductor lives
  with the loom that carries it, even when its signal belongs to a system.
- `components/`: one reusable type per file, grouped into `battery/`, `hv/` and
  `lv/`. Shared connector types are in `components/connectors.wb`.

When adding wiring, decide whether it is electrical intent, which goes in a
system, or a physical bundle, which goes in a loom. Shared rails such as safe
power and chassis ground are loose multi-endpoint wires, not cables.

## Conventions

- Format with `just wiring fmt`; `just wiring check` fails on unformatted
  files.
- Wire colours are IEC 60757, two-tone as `base/tracer`. CAN pairs are
  white/blue (H) and white/red (L); the minus leg of a feedback pair carries a
  black tracer.
- Every conductor between connectors must appear in a harness view
  (`undrawn_conductor`). When you add one, include both ends in the view of
  the loom that carries it.
- `just wiring check` is the test: there is no other suite. After layout,
  connector or harness changes, also run `just wiring serve` or
  `just wiring render` and look at the affected views.
- Commits take the `wiring` scope.
