Found in the review of ncmh.yf.

`TorqueMonitor::message` (`crates/vcm-core/src/monitor.rs:75-79`) resets its timeout on any CRC-valid `TorqueRequest` without checking that the message counter advances. A main MCU stuck resending one frame keeps the monitor satisfied. 6y59.ej covers torque against throttle, not the counter.

## Acceptance
- The monitor treats a request whose counter has not advanced as missing, so a frozen counter times out.
- Host tests: an advancing counter (including wrap-around) keeps the monitor alive; a repeated counter times out.
- Calls out the safety change in the commit. `just host ci` passes.
