## Steps
- Replace the STM32G491CC in `ecus/bms/hardware/master` with an STM32G474. Its 48-pin package is the smallest change if the pin count is enough.
- Update the `bms` wiki page's microcontroller section, which notes that the schematic still has the G491CC.

## Expected result
`just bms erc` passes with the new part.
