## Steps
- Replace the STM32G491CC in `ecus/bms/hardware/master` with the STM32G491CE. Same LQFP48 package and pinout, 512 KB instead of 256 KB, so the VCM main and the BMS masters share one bootloader layout.
- Update the `bms` wiki page's microcontroller section, which notes that the schematic still has the G491CC.

## Expected result
`just bms erc` passes with the new part.
