Found in the review of ncmh.yf.

The two pedal checks in `crates/vcm-core` disagree about which way sensor 2 moves:
- `CriticalInputs::throttle` (`src/input.rs:50-66`) treats sensor 2 as inverted (`-x + 1`) with a fixed 1 % tolerance.
- `Throttle` / `Map::new` (`src/throttle.rs:25-30`) sorts its bounds, so it cannot represent an inverted track. `ThrottleMonitor` uses it with a caller-set tolerance.

An inverted pedal at 25 % gives `SensorMismatch` in `Throttle`; a same-direction pedal at 25 % gives `Invalid` in `CriticalInputs`. Whatever the real pedal is, one check rejects a healthy pedal. The wiki (`monitor-firmware.md`, "Faulty accelerator signal") says 10 %.

## Acceptance
- One pedal model (track direction and voltage ranges per sensor) shared by the main and monitor checks, matching the pedal on the wiki. If the pedal's track direction isn't documented, record it as an open question and make the direction a parameter rather than guessing.
- One tolerance, taken from the wiki, or the wiki is updated with the reason.
- Host tests for a healthy pedal across its range, a disagreeing pair, and each sensor out of range, in both checks.
- Calls out the safety change in the commit. `just host ci` passes.
