Found in the review of ncmh.yf.

`monitor-firmware.md` says the Fault state is latched until reset. `ThrottleMonitor::check` (`crates/vcm-core/src/monitor.rs:38-49`) and `TorqueMonitor` (`monitor.rs:68-79`) return to Ok after one good reading or request. A pedal that is out of range every other tick never faults; the test `a_good_reading_resets_the_throttle_timeout` pins that behaviour.

## Acceptance
- The monitor logic in `vcm-core` latches a fault until an explicit reset (define what resets it, consistent with the wiki), or the wiki is changed with a documented reason.
- Host tests: an intermittent fault latches; a reset clears it; a latched fault stays latched through good readings.
- Calls out the safety change in the commit. `just host ci` passes.
